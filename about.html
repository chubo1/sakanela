Story

A place where the city was always in motion

The history of Sakanela began when Tbilisi was expanding toward modern-day Vera. In the late 19th and early 20th centuries, a network of streets and slopes gradually emerged here, connecting the heights of Vera to the banks of the Mtkvari River. Sakanela Descent was an integral part of this urban route: a gateway from the upper city down to the water’s edge.

Nearby lay Belle-Vue, one of the most prominent public gardens of old Tbilisi, while just a short distance away, at the top of Vera Descent, a historic velodrome hosted races. Thus, this place wove together the city’s daily rhythms, its constant movement, and its moments of unhurried leisure.

Over time, much of the historical architecture vanished, yet the significance of this location and its connection to the city remained untouched.

Masterplan

A place where the city was always in motion

Sakanela is situated on the right embankment of the Mtkvari River, adjacent to Republic Square, right where Vera, Rustaveli Avenue, the embankment, and Marjanishvili all converge.

As a result, the most vital parts of the city become effortlessly accessible in your day-to-day life.

Just a short walk or a few minutes' drive away:

  • Republic Square
  • Rustaveli Avenue
  • Vera
  • Marjanishvili Bridge

Lifestyle

A new
standard of City-centre
living

Rather than a single massive block, Sakanela is designed as seven distinct buildings distributed across two hectares.

The space between them is dedicated to inner courtyards, pedestrian lanes, landscaped green zones, and open plazas, lending the entire development a greater sense of openness and freedom.

This master plan creates inviting spots for both children and adults to relax, gather, and connect.

Apartments

Your space at Sakanela

Sakanela will feature 1-, 2-, and 3-bedroom apartments tailored to fit diverse lifestyles and needs. Depending on their position, the apartments offer panoramic views over the Mtkvari River, Mtatsminda, the historic districts of Tbilisi, and the city skyline.

Ceiling heights of 3 to 3.1 metres and expansive floor-to-ceiling windows fill the rooms with natural light, enhancing the sense of spaciousness and freedom.

The apartments will be delivered in green-frame condition. This will give owners the freedom to tailor the interiors to their individual tastes and lifestyles.
