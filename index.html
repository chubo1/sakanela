ENEN GEGE RURU

The city
around you

A place where
the city was always in motion

The history of Sakanela began when Tbilisi was expanding toward modern-day Vera.

In the late 19th and early 20th centuries, a network of streets and slopes gradually emerged here, connecting the heights of Vera to the banks of the Mtkvari River. Sakanela Descent was an integral part of this urban route: a gateway from the upper city down to the water’s edge.

Nearby lay Belle-Vue, one of the most prominent public gardens of old Tbilisi, while just a short distance away, at the top of Vera Descent, a historic velodrome hosted races. Thus, this place wove together the city’s daily rhythms, its constant movement, and its moments of unhurried leisure.

Location

At the centre
of the city

Sakanela is situated on the right embankment of the Mtkvari River, adjacent to Republic Square, right where Vera, Rustaveli Avenue, the embankment, and Marjanishvili all converge.

As a result, the most vital parts of the city become effortlessly accessible in your day-to-day life.

Just a short walk or a few minutes’ drive away:

  • Republic Square
  • Rustaveli Avenue
  • Vera
  • Marjanishvili Bridge
  • Mtkvari Embankment
  • Radisson Blu Iveria
  • Telegraph Hotel

Architecture by GRAFT

Tbilisi
Character, Contemporary
Architecture

The architecture of Sakanela was envisioned by the German studio GRAFT. The project’s central inspiration is the location itself: its rich history, the distinctive façades of old Tbilisi, traditional balconies, and complex architectural details.

Rather than directly replicating these historic elements, GRAFT reinterprets them in a universal language of contemporary architecture.
