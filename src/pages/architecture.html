Overview

Tbilisi character, contemporary architecture

The architecture of Sakanela was envisioned by the German studio GRAFT. The project’s central inspiration is the location itself: its rich history, the distinctive façades of old Tbilisi, traditional balconies, and complex architectural details. The architecture of Sakanela was envisioned by the German studio GRAFT. The project’s central inspiration is the location itself: its rich history, the distinctive façades of old Tbilisi, traditional balconies, and complex architectural details.

Rather than directly replicating these historic elements, GRAFT reinterprets them in a universal language of contemporary architecture.
